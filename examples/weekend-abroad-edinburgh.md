# Case study: a long weekend abroad on a budget

Anonymized from a real session. All prices checked on the day.

**Request:** a solo photographer wants a cheap city break abroad in early November, no vacation days, walking only, with a photography itinerary.

## Constraints discovered

- Saturday and Sunday free; Monday is a municipal holiday at the workplace; Tuesday is a remote-work day.
- Carry-on only; a camera backpack may exceed the free cabin item.
- New country for the traveler; entry authorization required for their nationality.

## Search

| Variant | Round trip (direct, low-cost) |
|---|---|
| Fri to Mon | about 194 |
| Sat to Mon | about 169 |
| **Sat to Tue** | **about 93** |

The notes from three weeks earlier said about 67 for Sat to Mon: stale.

## Decision

Sat to Tue at about 93. The Tuesday return at 06:00 lands at 09:55 so the traveler works from home. The last night is spent awake in the city with an open-return airport bus, which saved a third bed night.

## Accommodation

Hostel dorm of 6 beds in the old town, about 80 for two nights, picked over a 14-bed dorm for 78 and a 10-bed hostel with better reviews that listed a women-only bed. Luggage storage and late pickup were not published, so a message to the hostel was drafted.

## Things that changed the plan

- The authorization takes up to three working days: bought before anything else.
- A ceremony on the Sunday morning closes the main street.
- A viewpoint tower was announced as reopening the day before the check and still showed a closure notice: marked conditional.
- A free photography exhibition ended on the Sunday: scheduled that day.

## Deliverables

Purchase checklist with links, 60-plus calendar events with local times and map links, a "go now" link at the top of each event, and a pending list.
