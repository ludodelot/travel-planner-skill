# Prompt: photo and video itinerary for a city break

Copy, fill the brackets and paste into any assistant that can browse the web.

```
You are an expert travel photographer and videographer who knows <CITY> in detail. Build a day-by-day photo and video itinerary for a short solo trip. Use web browsing and verify anything time-sensitive (sunrise and sunset, opening hours, closures, viewpoint access, events on those dates). If you cannot verify something, label it "unverified" instead of guessing.

ABOUT ME
- Solo traveler, photographer AND videographer. I want as many iconic photo and video spots as possible, plus the best lesser-known angles.
- No car. I prefer walking between spots. Use transport only when walking makes no sense, and say so.
- Budget-conscious: prefer free viewpoints; tell me when a paid entry is worth it for the shot.
- Gear: <camera, lens range, gimbal/tripod, phone>.

TRIP LOGISTICS (fixed)
- Dates: <dates>.
- Arrival: <day, time, airport>. Plan nothing intense that night.
- Staying at: <accommodation, area>.
- Full days: <days>. Check-out time and luggage storage: <details>.
- Departure: <day, time>. Last night: <sleeping or not, transfer plan>.
- Daylight is short in <season>: plan around sunrise, sunset, blue hour and night.

DELIVER
1. Overview: how to split the days by area to minimize walking.
2. Timeline per day with start times, walking time and distance from the previous stop, and best light.
3. For every spot: exact viewpoint, why it is iconic, best time and light, photo ideas with focal lengths, video ideas, crowds, cost, tripod or filming rules.
4. At least <25-30> spots, prioritized by value per walking minute.
5. A rain version of each day.
6. Practical notes: safety at night, warm cheap food near each stop, battery charging, cold-weather battery tips.
7. A final table: spot, area, best time, walking minutes from where I sleep, photo and video value (1 to 5).

STYLE
Concise, structured, tables where useful. Most valuable shots first. Flag anything unverified.
```
