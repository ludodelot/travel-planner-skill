# Transport: flights, trains, buses and transfers

## Train vs. flight

| Factor | Train | Flight |
|---|---|---|
| Real door-to-door time | Station to station, little buffer | Add airport transfer, security, bag drop, arrival transfer (about 3 extra hours on a short hop) |
| Departure flexibility | Work until late and leave in the evening | Early flights force early mornings |
| Price risk | Cheapest fares sell out; "from" prices on aggregators | Low-cost fares jump close to bridges and holidays |
| Baggage | Usually generous | Fees decide the real price |

For trips under about 5 hours by rail, compare both on **total cost and total time**.

## Rail checklist

- Confirm the fare on the operator's checkout; aggregators show "from" prices.
- Fare types: the cheapest is often non-refundable with one paid change. A slightly higher flexible fare (free change, partial refund) is cheap insurance when plans can move.
- Read notices on the service. Speed restrictions or works can mean delays that are not compensated.
- Compare arrival time with the accommodation's check-in hours.

## Low-cost airline checklist

- Free cabin item size (for example 40 x 30 x 20 cm) vs. a camera backpack; price the "priority and 2 bags" add-on per leg.
- Online check-in is mandatory if you do not drop a bag; keep the boarding pass offline on the phone.
- Boarding gates close well before departure; plan the airport arrival accordingly.
- Early-morning flights: arrange the transfer first. Check that night buses run and when the airport security opens.

## Airport transfers

- Look for a 24-hour airport bus or an open-return ticket; an open return can save around a third versus two singles.
- Confirm the stop for the way back and any temporary relocation of the stop.
- Keep a plan B departure that leaves more margin.

## Night and early departures

If the traveler stays up the last night: plan warm, open places near the stop with long hours, a power bank, a light backpack, and avoid isolated areas. Reconfirm opening hours the week of travel; closing times change.

## Intercity buses for bridges

Short buses between nearby cities are cheap but can sell out around holidays. Buy them the week before, not the day before. Use a transit directions link to see companies and times, then the operator site to buy.
