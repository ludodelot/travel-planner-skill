# Researching a destination efficiently

Do this for the shortlist only, after prices and constraints are verified.

## What to find, in this order

1. **Fit**: does it match the traveler's interests and pace? One paragraph, no tourism-brochure language.
2. **Season and light**: sunrise, sunset and twilight for the exact dates (`scripts/sun.py`), typical weather, daylight hours for the planned activities.
3. **Hard facts that break plans**: holidays and ceremonies on the dates, weekly closing days, works or closures announced, reservation requirements, strikes, events that raise prices.
4. **Getting around**: airport or station to the center (night options, tickets, an open return), walking distances between clusters, last transport of the day.
5. **Where to stay**: areas, not just properties (`accommodation.md`).
6. **What is free**: viewpoints, museums, galleries, terraces, markets, temporary exhibitions that end on a date.
7. **Safety and etiquette**: areas to avoid at night, scams, filming rules (tripods, drones), dress codes.

## Sources, best to worst

| Source | Use for |
|---|---|
| Official tourism board, city council, operator, venue | Hours, closures, tickets, rules |
| Official government pages | Entry requirements, holidays |
| Local photographers and community guides | Viewpoints, timing, angles |
| Aggregators and reviews | Prices, ratings, recent complaints |
| Social media | Inspiration only; verify everything |

## Verification habits

- Open the venue page for the exact weekday before putting a stop in the plan.
- Look for a "closed for maintenance until" note and compare it with the date you are checking: the reopening date often slips.
- Mark anything you could not verify and put a dated reminder to recheck it the week before.

## Output

A one-screen destination card: why it fits, dates and light, three risks, getting around, where to stay, the free highlights, and a list of unverified items. Then hand off to `itinerary-design.md`.

## Efficiency

- Search for the three or four facts that can break the plan before reading anything else.
- Prefer one good official page over five blog posts.
- Do not research destinations that failed a hard constraint (holiday closure, entry rule, price spike).
