# Choosing accommodation (hostels and budget stays)

## Criteria, in priority order

1. **Hard rules from the traveler**: dorm size cap, mixed vs. single-gender, private bathroom, free cancellation, breakfast.
2. **Arrival and departure hours**: 24-hour reception if arriving at night or leaving at 2 to 3 am; luggage storage if there is a gap between check-out and departure.
3. **Location**: walking distance to the main clusters beats a small saving. Count the cost of transport back late at night.
4. **Rating and recent reviews**: read the latest comments on cleanliness and noise, especially for party areas.
5. **Price** for the whole stay with taxes, in the same currency.

## Traps seen in practice

| Trap | What happened | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Single-gender dorm | The best-rated hostel's cheapest bed was in a women-only dorm | Read the room name (mixed vs. female) before shortlisting |
| "From" prices | A city page showed a minimum price that ignored the dates | Compare only prices with dates in the URL |
| Big dorms | Cheapest beds were in 10, 14 or 18-bed rooms | Filter by dorm size from the room name |
| Late arrival | Train arrives at 23:00, reception hours unknown | Ask or check "24-hour reception" |
| No luggage storage | Planned a late departure after check-out | Confirm storage, price and pickup hours by message before booking |
| No free cancellation | Cheapest rate was non-refundable | Prefer free-cancellation rates unless the saving is large and plans are fixed |

## Booking.com vs. Hostelworld

- **Booking**: dates and the hostel filter work in the URL; prices include taxes; the room name shows dorm size; free cancellation is flagged.
- **Hostelworld**: good for ratings and vibe; the city page may ignore dates and show minimum prices. Use it for reviews, and verify the stay price by entering dates manually.
- Always get the **direct property URL** from the results and append the dates before sharing.

## Extraction template

For each candidate: name, total for the stay, bed type and dorm size, rating (count), distance to center, free cancellation yes/no, breakfast, 24-hour reception yes/no/unknown, URL with dates.

## Messages worth sending to the property

- Luggage storage after check-out: price, and whether the bag can be collected at night.
- Late or very early arrival and departure.
- Use of the common area to charge devices between check-out and departure.

Draft the message; let the traveler send it.

## Short stays and night transfers

When the traveler will not sleep the last night, a bed for two nights plus luggage storage can beat a third night. Plan an indoor waiting spot with long opening hours and charging, and a safe route to the stop.
