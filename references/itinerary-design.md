# Designing walking itineraries (photo and video friendly)

## Principles

1. **Cluster by walking, not by fame.** Group stops by neighborhood; sort by value per walking minute.
2. **Light first.** Check sunrise, sunset and civil twilight for the exact dates and build the day around them: exteriors in good light, museums and interiors in the dead hours, night scenes after dark.
3. **Fewer, better.** Two or three protected "golden" sessions a day beat ten rushed stops. Mark stops as `core`, `optional` or `cut if late`.
4. **Buffers.** Add 5 to 10 minutes between stops and a meal break; a 25-stop day is fine only if most stops are two to five minutes.
5. **Have a rain plan** per day: indoor free attractions, covered passages, galleries, reflections on wet streets.
6. **Verify the fragile things**: opening hours for the exact weekday, closures for works, holidays and ceremonies, reservation needs, restricted paths.

## Per-stop card

- Name and exact viewpoint, not just the district.
- Why it is worth it.
- Best light and time slot.
- Photo ideas with focal-length ranges; video ideas (gimbal walk, timelapse, slow pan, detail b-roll).
- Walking distance and minutes from the previous stop.
- Cost and rules (tripods, flash, filming).
- Crowds and safety.

## Typical daily shape (short November-type days)

| Block | Use |
|---|---|
| First light to mid-morning | Streets and viewpoints with few people |
| Midday | Free museums, galleries, terraces, lunch |
| Afternoon | Move to the sunset viewpoint with a margin |
| Sunset and blue hour | Protected session, tripod only if allowed |
| Evening | Warm meal, backup photos, charging, rest |

## Night sessions

- Use lit main streets, avoid isolated viewpoints after dark, keep the camera in front, never leave bags unattended.
- Starting settings: wide aperture, auto ISO, 1/125 s or faster with people, video at 4K 25 fps with a shutter around 1/50 s.
- End near a transport stop or the accommodation.

## Free culture to look for

Free national museums, rooftop terraces, galleries with temporary shows (check start and end dates), university campuses, gardens, markets. Exhibitions that end on a given day are worth building a day around.

## Things that break plans

- Remembrance or religious ceremonies and processions closing main streets.
- Seasonal works (holiday markets) that block viewpoints before the official opening date.
- Paths closed for rockfall or ice.
- Attractions closed for maintenance beyond the announced date: confirm by phone or the venue page the week before.
- Weekly closing days of galleries.

## Budget block

List only what is not already paid: transport pass, paid viewpoints, food by day, lockers. Give a range and a margin.
