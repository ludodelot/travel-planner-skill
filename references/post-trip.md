# After the trip: close the loop

A short routine that makes the next plan better.

## Within a few days of returning

1. **Actuals vs. plan**: what was paid, in the currency it was paid, against the budget table. Note the differences and why.
2. **What worked and what did not**: stops that were worth the walk, stops cut for time, timings that were too tight, weather surprises, closures found on the spot.
3. **Prices and rules observed**: add any fact that changes the next recommendation (for example, a fare pattern, an operator notice, an entry rule that differed from what was expected). Date every fact.
4. **Lessons** go to `references/lessons-learned.md` only if they generalize; personal preferences go to the traveler's own notes.
5. **Update the travel log**: visited places, new countries, dates.
6. **Content**: if the traveler creates content (photos, stories, posts), schedule the editing and publishing block in the calendar.

## Questions to ask the traveler

- Would you do this combination again? What would you change?
- Which day was best, and which stop was the best for the effort?
- Was the budget about right? Where did you overspend?
- Any new constraint or preference to remember?

## Clean-up

- Mark calendar events as done or remove obsolete optional items.
- Confirm refunds or credits that were expected (cancelled optional bookings).
- Delete temporary notes with sensitive details; keep only what the playbook says to keep.

## Feeding the skill

Add a short, anonymized case study to `examples/` when the trip taught something new: constraints, the comparison that decided it, what changed the plan, what was learned. No names, no employers, no booking references.
